The starting point

How do you make a museum story something a family can join in with? I explored a browser experience that mixes short chapters, spoken storytelling, questions and drawing.

A story with room to play

The prototype moves between story beats and moments where visitors can respond. Questions invite their own ideas, while colouring and drawing give children another way into the story.

Making it practical

I worked through QR access, mobile browsers, accessibility and testing. I also compared a static site with a backend for saving responses. When stored answers weren’t needed, the simpler approach made more sense.

Where it stands

The prototype demonstrates the story and interaction flow. Next, I’d test it with families in the exhibition to see where the experience is clear and where they need help.
