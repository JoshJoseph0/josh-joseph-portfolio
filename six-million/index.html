Reading the memorial

Scale, evidence, individual lives.

One dot is one statistical unit within the estimate. The field has a finite boundary and exactly six million stable positions. You begin inside it. At most 0.45% of the field can be in view at once. The minimap shows your place within the whole; move closer to encounter individual marks.

Approximately six million Jewish people were murdered by Nazi Germany and its allies and collaborators during the Holocaust.

The six million slots here give a fixed visual form to a historical estimate. They are not six million individually verified archival records, and their positions have no geographical meaning.

  • Red — approximately five-sixths, reflecting the share of names documented by Yad Vashem. A red dot is not proof that Six Million holds a biography for that position.
  • Grey — the remaining share of the statistical estimate. This does not establish that an individual is historically unidentified.

These are approximate aggregate proportions, not a mapping of archival names to particular dots. Each dot starts unassigned. When the shared service is connected, selecting any dot permanently associates an available approved person with it in the database. Every visitor then sees that same person at that dot. Colours remain aggregate statistical proportions and do not indicate assignment status.

Project methodology and limitations

Reading the field

A finite estimate. Individual evidence.

Exactly 6,000,000 deterministic statistical positions form a staggered plane of 3,000 columns and 2,000 rows. Small, bounded offsets are fixed by each slot ID. Their positions are not geographical.

The scale indicator counts the actual jittered dot centres inside the canvas viewport, including those behind interface text. It measures the visible spatial window, not biography coverage or a task to complete.

At distant zoom, average circle area and aggregate red/grey proportions produce a density image. Intermediate levels use bounded representative point batches. Only close zoom resolves the true individual positions. The overview does not claim six million dots are individually distinguishable.

Only source-checked approved profiles can be associated with a dot. With the shared service connected, assignments are shared across visitors and saved permanently in the database. External discoveries and live Wikidata claims cannot approve themselves. Historical source checks are AI-assisted; independent historian review has not been performed.

Photographs require a separate image-rights record, attribution and permission or a documented public-domain/open-licence basis. A public archive page alone does not clear reuse.
