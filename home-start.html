The organisation

Home-Start Watford & Three Rivers supports local families. Its website needs to help people understand the charity, find support and discover ways to get involved.

My contribution

I have contributed volunteer website work and ongoing maintenance since 2022. It is a continuing commitment to a real organisation, alongside my studies and other projects.

Design as upkeep

A charity website needs to remain useful after the first version. This work has given me experience contributing to an existing site and keeping an organisation’s online presence current.

Where it stands

My involvement is ongoing. The live website is the best place to see its current content. This case study focuses on my website contribution; it makes no claims about traffic growth or service outcomes.
