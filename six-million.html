Six Million

Creative coding experiments exploring how to make six million points understandable. I’m prototyping ways to move between the overall field and individual marks using pan and zoom.

The starting point

A very large number is easy to write down and hard to understand. I started exploring what it feels like to encounter that scale as a field of individual points.

The idea becomes a rendering problem

As the field grows, drawing every mark at every scale becomes expensive. The experiments brought together composition, pan and zoom, rendering behaviour and performance testing.

Why interaction matters

A useful interaction should help someone move between the overall scale and individual marks. I’m interested in that change in understanding, as well as whether the browser can draw the result.

Where it stands

These are exploratory prototypes. The image above illustrates the idea; it is not a live rendering of six million points. Next, I’d compare rendering approaches and test how clearly pan and zoom communicate the scale.
