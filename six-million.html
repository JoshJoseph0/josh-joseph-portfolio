The starting point

Six million is a number with a specific historical weight: the Jewish people murdered in the Holocaust. I began exploring how a field of individual marks could encourage reflection on that scale, while recognising that a mark cannot represent the full life of a person.

Representing scale with care

As the field grows, drawing every mark at every scale becomes expensive. The experiments brought together composition, pan and zoom, rendering behaviour and performance testing.

Interaction in service of remembrance

Pan and zoom could allow a slow movement between the field and its individual marks. The intention is remembrance and reflection. Historical context needs to stay visible, and interaction should support it without scores, rewards or spectacle.

Where it stands

These are exploratory prototypes. The image above illustrates the idea; it is not a live rendering of six million points. Next, I’d compare rendering approaches and test how clearly pan and zoom communicate the scale.
